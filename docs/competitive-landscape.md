# Competitive landscape (November 2026)

A wave of "AI climbing coach" apps appeared in 2025–2026. Video analysis on its own is no longer
a differentiator; the opportunity is in *how* feedback fits real gym habits.

## Technique analysis from video

| App | What it does | Notes for us |
|---|---|---|
| **BetterClimber** | real-time pose tracking, skeleton replay, imports existing videos; all processing on-device, no account | closest to this engine; privacy-first is a strong pattern to keep |
| **VBeta** | maps body position, detects common bouldering issues and surfaces *the one* that hurt the send; token-based pricing | validates the "one main issue" principle |
| **Bouldering AI: Coach** | video feedback on body position, footwork, hip angle; chat coach, training and nutrition plans | broad "coach" scope, subscription |
| **Climbah, Rockover, klettra** | logging + AI video feedback + training plans | crowded "log + AI" space |

## Setting & sharing boulders on wall photos

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Stōkt** | the leader for spray / gym walls: photo → automatic hold detection → select holds → publish; crowd grading in Hueco, Font, DanKyu |
| **Sprai, Boardclimbs, OpenClimb** | similar photo-based setting with hold detection |
| **BoulderBot** | generates problems on your wall |

## Research

  novice errors; key idea that an error depends on the climbing phase.
- *Using Pose Estimation to Analyze Rock Climbing Technique* – Stanford CS231n, 2024.
- *Computer Vision Based Indoor Rock Climbing Analysis* – UCSD.

## Gaps this project aims at

1. **Real filming conditions** – hand-held, zooming, partial occlusion; most tools assume a fixed camera.
2. **Linking analysis to a specific boulder and hold** – technique apps and setting apps are separate worlds.
3. **Gym-specific grades** – translating a gym's own colour / level system into Font or V-scale for visitors.
4. **Trust & tone** – visible confidence, the ability to say "that was intentional", tone adapted to beginners and kids.
